Product brief · August 2026

A faster way to finish AI-generated documents

AI can get a document ninety percent of the way there. The last ten percent should feel like editing, not another conversation.

Small corrections should take seconds. Select the text on the rendered page, make the change directly, and save only that source fragment back to the original HTML.

Product principle

Keep the application quiet enough that every document can retain its own visual identity. Editing controls should be immediately available, then disappear when they are not needed.
